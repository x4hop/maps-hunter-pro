# Maps Hunter Pro — SEO + YouTube Organic Growth Plan (2026)

## 1. Technical SEO now implemented

Indexable language routes:
- `/en`
- `/ar`
- `/ru`
- `/de`
- `/es`

Each route receives its own server-side title, meta description, `lang`, `dir`, canonical URL, Open Graph metadata and `hreflang` links. `/` permanently redirects to `/en`. `robots.txt` points to `sitemap.xml`, and the sitemap includes all five language routes plus Privacy and Terms.

Google guidance to preserve:
- Keep self-referencing canonicals on each language URL.
- Keep reciprocal `hreflang` between all localized versions.
- Do not canonicalize every language back to English.
- Keep the main page content genuinely localized, not just the header/footer.
- Search Console should be used to inspect Google-selected canonicals after production-domain launch.

## 3. Landing-page on-page rules

- One clear H1 centered on the problem and output: Google Maps → organized business leads.
- Mention Excel/CSV/JSON naturally in feature and export sections.
- Demonstrate international phone formatting with real country-code examples.
- Keep pricing visible and crawlable as text.
- FAQ should answer activation, limits, export formats and phone formatting.
- Do not claim hands-on features that do not exist.
- Keep Privacy, Terms, Refund and Acceptable Use linked from every language page.
- Add real product screenshots/video once available; these improve trust and conversion more than decorative stock images.

## 8. Publishing cadence

Launch phase:
- 2 long-form videos/week for the first 8 weeks.
- 2–4 Shorts cut from each long-form tutorial.
- Keep one primary search intent per long video.
- Update weak thumbnails/titles after enough impressions accumulate; do not constantly change metadata before useful data exists.

Suggested weekly pattern:
- Tuesday: search-driven tutorial.
- Friday: niche workflow/comparison.
- Shorts: 24–72 hours after each long-form video.

## 9. Thumbnail rules

- 2–4 words maximum.
- Show Maps/Excel/product UI, not generic stock art.
- Use one strong visual transformation: search → leads, Maps → Excel, manual → automated workflow.
- Make text readable on a phone.
- Avoid fake numbers/results that the video does not demonstrate.

## 10. Measurement

Website:
- Search Console clicks, impressions, CTR and average position by language route.
- Queries where position is 5–20 receive title/content refinement first.
- Track indexed canonical URL and `hreflang` behavior.

YouTube:
- Search impressions.
- CTR by traffic source.
- Average percentage viewed.
- First 30-second retention.
- Watch time from YouTube Search.
- Product-page clicks per video.

Decision rule: a video with high impressions but low CTR needs title/thumbnail work; high CTR but poor retention needs content/hook work.
