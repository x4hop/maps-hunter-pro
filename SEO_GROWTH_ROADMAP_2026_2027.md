# Maps Hunter Pro — SEO Growth Roadmap 2026–2027

## Goal
Build Maps Hunter Pro into a multilingual, commercially useful content authority around Google Maps business research and lead generation, with search traffic that converts into paid extension users.

## Core principles
- People-first content before keyword volume.
- Every article must solve a real task, answer a real decision, or provide a real workflow.
- English, Arabic, German, Spanish, and Russian are first-class SEO markets.
- No thin city doorway pages, mass spun translations, fake statistics, unsupported speed/accuracy claims, or fabricated testimonials.
- Commercial pages use factual comparison tables with a visible “pricing checked” date.
- Every important article links naturally to Maps Hunter Pro pricing/purchase CTA near the top, middle, and end.
- Every important page must receive at least one contextual internal link from another page.
- Images are created only when they add explanatory or conversion value; use descriptive filenames/alt text and real article image metadata.

## Competitor content program
Tier 1 comparison pages:
1. Maps Hunter Pro vs PhantomBuster
2. Maps Hunter Pro vs Outscraper
3. Maps Hunter Pro vs Bright Data
4. Maps Hunter Pro vs Apify Google Maps Scraper
5. Maps Hunter Pro vs ScrapeHero
6. Best Google Maps Lead Generation Tools — comparison hub

Comparison rules:
- Verify pricing and plan limits from the competitor's official website on publication day.
- Separate “focused Google Maps workflow” from “broader automation/data infrastructure”.
- Never claim Maps Hunter Pro is faster without a repeatable benchmark.
- Use “lower fixed subscription price” only where current public prices support it.
- Explain where the competitor is genuinely stronger; this increases trust and makes the recommendation useful.

## Multilingual strategy
Supported markets: EN, AR, DE, ES, RU.
- Use separate locale URLs.
- Add self-canonical + reciprocal hreflang only for pages that actually exist.
- Localize examples, headings, CTA language and intent; do not publish literal translations without editorial adaptation.
- Priority: commercial comparison pages and evergreen tutorials first.
- Language rotation for daily publishing: EN → AR → DE → ES → RU, while high-value commercial pages receive localized versions across all five languages.

## 90-day execution
### Days 1–14
- Publish 5 competitor comparison pages.
- Create comparison hub.
- Publish/finish language hubs.
- Ensure sitemap and hreflang coverage.
- Add article CTA components and consistent comparison tables.
- Add real author/editorial methodology page and pricing-check policy.

### Days 15–45
- Publish 20–30 problem-solving tutorials across the five language markets.
- Build agency + web design + local SEO clusters.
- Refresh older articles with reciprocal internal links.
- Add useful screenshots/diagrams where they improve understanding.
- Start outreach/distribution to relevant communities, tutorial videos and product directories.

### Days 46–90
- Expand into SaaS, supplier, recruiter, real-estate and vertical-specific clusters.
- Consolidate cannibalizing pages.
- Update comparison prices monthly.
- Improve pages with impressions but weak CTR by testing titles/descriptions.
- Improve pages ranking positions 8–30 with deeper sections, examples and internal links.
- Build backlinks through original data studies and genuinely useful templates/checklists.

## Conversion framework
Every article should contain:
1. Contextual CTA after the problem is established.
2. Mid-article CTA after the workflow or comparison.
3. Bottom CTA with clear price/value proposition.
4. Internal link to pricing and at least two related guides.
5. No aggressive popups that degrade reading.

## KPI dashboard

- Organic impressions/clicks
- Non-brand clicks
- Top queries by language
- Pages in positions 4–20
- CTR by page/query
- Organic visits to pricing
- CTA clicks from blog
- Purchases/activation inquiries attributed to organic landing pages

Monthly:
- Traffic and conversions by content cluster
- Comparison-page conversion rate
- Organic revenue by language
- Winning/losing URLs
- Content refresh queue
- Competitor pricing freshness

## Success target
The target is not “100 articles”. The target is a growing library where every indexed page has a clear search intent, useful content, internal links, and a path to purchase. Traffic goals should be updated from real Search Console performance rather than fabricated search-volume forecasts.
