FAQ

Before you start.

What is Maps Hunter Pro?

A productivity tool for discovering and organizing local business information from map-based searches for legitimate prospecting and market research workflows.

Do I renew using the same email?

Yes. The planned account flow uses the email registered on the website to identify the customer and their license during renewal.

How does the affiliate program work?

The launch plan allocates a 20% commission on eligible renewals. Full tracking, attribution, payout thresholds and anti-fraud rules will be published before the affiliate portal goes live.

Is the activation system live?

Not yet. This first release is the public frontend foundation. Checkout, license validation and account APIs are the next production layer.
