Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Monthly and Annual?

Monthly is $20 with up to 1,500 leads per day. Annual is $100 per year with no commercial daily platform limit.

Are phone numbers exported internationally?

Yes. Phone numbers can be formatted according to the country being searched.

How do I receive the activation code?

Send your payment proof and transaction reference on WhatsApp. After verification, your activation code is sent manually.

Does every business include an email or social profile?

No. Email and social fields are included when they are available in the supported source data.

Which browser does Maps Hunter Pro support?

Maps Hunter Pro is built as a Chrome extension.

Can I export Google Maps leads to Excel, CSV or JSON?

Yes. Results can be exported to Excel, CSV, JSON, or viewed in the Results table.
