Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Data we process

We process account name and email, salted password hashes, sessions, activation and payment references, license and device identifiers, daily usage totals, affiliate attribution, and security logs. We do not need or upload the lead lists you export from Google Maps.

Why

This data is used to provide accounts, manual activation through WhatsApp, subscriptions, device limits, support, abuse prevention, affiliate commissions, and service security.

Storage and security

Passwords are never stored as plaintext. Sessions and administrative credentials are protected server-side. Access is restricted to service operation and support.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of account data, subject to records we must retain for fraud prevention, accounting, or legal obligations.
