Privacy Policy

Effective: September 18, 2026

Maps Hunter Pro uses manual payment verification and activation codes. There is no customer website registration, login, or password-recovery flow.

Extension data

Business lead lists, extraction queues and derived contact fields are stored locally in the browser and are not uploaded to the Maps Hunter Pro licensing API. You can clear saved results from the extension or remove extension storage by uninstalling the extension.

Maps-only contact extraction

Maps Hunter Pro extracts public business fields from the Google Maps results and detail pages selected by the user. If Google Maps itself exposes an email address, the extension can save it with the result. The extension does not open or fetch the business website to search for email addresses or social links.

Licensing and usage

To validate access and enforce product limits, the extension sends the activation code, a random installation identifier, an idempotent usage request identifier and usage count to the licensing service. Customer activation codes are stored server-side as hashes. Cloudflare may provide standard connection information such as IP address for security and abuse prevention.

Payment verification

Payments are handled manually. A customer may send a payment screenshot and transaction reference/address through WhatsApp so the operator can verify the payment and issue an activation code. Maps Hunter Pro does not require a customer website account for this process.

Activation codes

Monthly codes begin a 30-day term on first successful activation and may be renewed manually. Lifetime codes have no expiry unless access is revoked for an applicable enforcement or support reason. Customer codes are limited to one trusted device; the operator can reset a legitimate device binding when needed.

Purpose and limited use

Data is used only for the disclosed business-research, export, licensing, usage-limit, support and security functions. We do not sell extension user data or use it for unrelated advertising or profiling.

Your choices

You control the Google Maps searches you run and can clear locally stored extraction results. For privacy or support requests, contact Maps Hunter Pro through WhatsApp.

Independence

Maps Hunter Pro is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or an official product of Google.
