Privacy Policy

Effective: September 19, 2026

Maps Hunter Pro uses manual payment verification and activation codes. There is no customer website registration, login, or password-recovery flow.

Extension data

Business lead lists, extraction queues and derived contact fields are stored locally in the browser and are not uploaded to the Maps Hunter Pro licensing API.

Public website contact enrichment

Maps Hunter Pro first extracts public business fields and contact data exposed by Google Maps. When a selected business includes an official website, the extension may fetch publicly accessible pages from that website in its background service worker to locate public business email addresses and social-profile links. The website is not opened as a visible tab. Raw website pages are processed transiently and are not uploaded to the Maps Hunter Pro licensing API.

Licensing and usage

The extension sends the activation code, a random installation identifier, an idempotent usage request identifier and usage count to the licensing service. Customer activation codes are stored server-side as hashes and customer codes are limited to one trusted device.

Subscription terms

Monthly codes begin a 30-day term on first successful activation. Lifetime codes have no expiry unless revoked.

Purpose and limited use

Data is used only for the disclosed business-research, export, licensing, usage-limit, support and security functions. We do not sell extension user data or use it for unrelated advertising or profiling.

Your choices

You control the searches you run and can clear locally stored extraction results. For support or privacy requests, contact Maps Hunter Pro through WhatsApp.

Independence

Maps Hunter Pro is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or an official product of Google.
