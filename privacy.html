Privacy Policy

Effective: September 11, 2026

Maps Hunter Pro uses manual payment verification and activation codes. There is no customer account, registration, customer login, or password system on the website.

Extension data

Business lead lists stay in the browser and are not uploaded to our licensing API. To validate an activation code and enforce product limits, the extension sends the activation code, a random installation identifier, an idempotent usage request identifier, and usage count. Cloudflare may provide standard connection information such as IP address for security and abuse prevention.

Payment verification

Payments are handled manually. A customer may send a payment screenshot and transaction reference/address through WhatsApp so the operator can verify the payment and issue or extend an activation code. Maps Hunter Pro does not require a website customer account for this process.

Activation codes

Customer activation codes are validated by the server. New codes begin their subscription term on first successful activation. The extension displays the expiry date. Codes may be revoked, extended, or have device bindings reset by the operator when necessary.

Purpose and limited use

Data is used only to provide the disclosed licensing, usage-limit, support, security, and business-research functions. We do not sell extension user data or use it for unrelated advertising or profiling.

Your choices

You can clear locally stored extraction results from the extension. For privacy or support requests, contact Maps Hunter Pro through WhatsApp.

Independence

Maps Hunter Pro is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or an official product of Google.
