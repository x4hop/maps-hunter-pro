Privacy Policy

Effective: September 8, 2026

This policy describes the real behavior of the Maps Hunter Pro Chrome extension, website, account service and manual activation service operated by Maps Hunter Pro. Contact: WhatsApp +218 93 165 0822.

Extension data and local storage

When you press Start, the extension reads public business information visible in the Google Maps pages you selected: business name, public phone, address, website, image, category, rating, review count, hours and Maps URL. If you enable optional website enrichment and approve Chrome's website permission, it may visit the business website and common contact pages to find public email addresses and social links. Results, queues, settings and recovery state are stored in chrome.storage.local. They remain until you clear results, remove extension data or uninstall. Clear Results removes saved results and unfinished work; downloaded exports remain on your device.

Data sent to our service

Business lead lists are not uploaded to our API. For licensing and limits, the extension sends the license key, random installation identifier, idempotent request identifier, operating-system/browser labels and usage count. Cloudflare supplies connection data such as IP address for security, device limits and abuse prevention. The website processes name, email, salted password derivations, sessions, plan and activation requests, payment references, affiliate attribution and support/audit records.

Service providers and destinations

  • Cloudflare: hosts the website, Workers API and D1 database and processes technical connection data.
  • Google Maps: receives searches and normal browser requests when you use Maps under Google's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Business websites: receive ordinary connection information only when optional enrichment is enabled.
  • WhatsApp/Meta: receives the message and information you choose to send for manual activation or support. Exported lead lists are not automatically sent to WhatsApp.

Purpose and Limited Use

We use data only to provide the disclosed business-research, organization, export, account, licensing, billing-support, affiliate, security and recovery functions. We do not sell extension user data or use it for advertising, credit decisions or unrelated profiling. Our use and transfer of user data is limited to the disclosed purpose and complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. Human access is limited to necessary support, payment verification, fraud/security investigation and legal obligations.

Retention

Browser results remain until you clear them. Sessions expire automatically; expired sessions, rate-limit records and raw usage events are periodically removed. Account and license records remain while the account is active. Payment, commission, payout and related audit records may be retained for reconciliation, fraud prevention or legal obligations; deletion requests anonymize nonessential identity instead of erasing required financial history.

Your choices and requests

You can decline or revoke optional website access and continue using the core Maps workflow. You can clear local results at any time. For access, correction or deletion, sign in and submit a data request from your account, or contact support from the registered email/number. We verify ownership before acting and explain any records that must be retained.

Security and independence

Passwords are not stored in plaintext. Server sessions are hashed and expire. A paid account and manual payment are independent of a Google account. Maps Hunter Pro is not endorsed by, affiliated with or an official product of Google.

Children and changes

The service is intended for business users and not children. Material changes will be published here with a new effective date.
