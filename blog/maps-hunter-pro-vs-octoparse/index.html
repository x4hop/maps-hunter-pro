Maps Hunter Pro vs Octoparse: Which Fits Your Google Maps Lead Workflow?

Maps Hunter Pro is a focused Chrome extension for turning Google Maps business research into organized exports. Octoparse is a broader no-code web scraping platform with local and cloud extraction, scheduling, templates and APIs on paid tiers. The right choice depends on whether you need a prospecting tool or a general scraping platform.

Need Google Maps prospecting without building a scraper?

Maps Hunter Pro is built around one job: collect supported Google Maps business data, review it and export clean lead lists from Chrome.

Quick answer

Choose Maps Hunter Pro when Google Maps lead research is the main job and you want a focused Chrome workflow with Excel, CSV, JSON and Results-table exports.

Choose Octoparse when you need a general no-code scraper for many websites, recurring cloud jobs, task scheduling, anti-blocking infrastructure or API-driven workflows.

Octoparse is not simply a more expensive version of Maps Hunter Pro. It solves a broader class of scraping problems. Maps Hunter Pro deliberately removes much of that platform complexity for users whose work starts and ends with Google Maps prospecting.

Pricing: compare the job, not just the plan

Maps Hunter Pro currently costs $20/month for up to 1,500 results per day, or $100 one-time for lifetime access with no commercial daily platform limit. Those are product limits and pricing published on the Maps Hunter Pro site.

Octoparse has a free-forever plan. Its current pricing page says the free plan includes 10 tasks, local extraction on one device and up to 50,000 rows of monthly data export. Paid Standard starts from $69/month when billed annually, while Professional is $249/month when billed annually. Octoparse's 2026 pricing guide also lists month-to-month Standard from $83 and Professional at $299. Enterprise pricing is custom.

That does not mean Maps Hunter Pro is always cheaper for every project. Octoparse's free plan can be enough for learning, testing or small local scraping tasks. But if your recurring job is specifically Google Maps prospecting, compare the cost and setup of the workflow you actually need rather than paying for general scraping capabilities you may never use.

Focused Maps workflow vs no-code scraper builder

Maps Hunter Pro

The workflow is operator-led: search Google Maps, collect supported business fields, review results, format phone numbers and export the list. The product is designed for salespeople, agencies, freelancers and researchers who want business leads rather than a general crawler-building environment.

Octoparse

Octoparse is a no-code web scraping platform. You create or use scraping tasks, run them locally or—on appropriate paid plans—in the cloud, and extract structured data from many kinds of websites. Octoparse also offers hundreds of preset templates and paid add-ons for proxies, CAPTCHA solving and some pay-per-result templates.

If your team regularly needs to scrape product catalogs, marketplaces, news sites and other web pages in addition to Maps, Octoparse's broader model can be a major advantage. If the requirement is simply to build local-business prospect lists from Google Maps, that breadth may add setup and features you do not need.

Cloud extraction, scheduling and API

This is where Octoparse has the stronger platform capability. Its Standard plan includes cloud extraction, task scheduling, IP rotation and Data Export API access. Professional expands concurrency and adds advanced API capabilities plus cloud-storage integrations such as Google Sheets/Drive, Dropbox and S3 according to Octoparse's current plan documentation.

The free Octoparse plan does not include cloud extraction, scheduling or API access. Those features become relevant when scraping needs to run unattended or feed another system.

Maps Hunter Pro is not positioned as a cloud scraping API. Its advantage is the opposite: it keeps the Google Maps research process close to the person qualifying the leads.

Do you need automation infrastructure—or a lead tool?

If your sales workflow is human-led and centered on Google Maps, a focused Chrome product can be easier to operate. If your backend must schedule jobs and retrieve data through APIs, Octoparse is the more natural category.

Exports and data handling

Maps Hunter Pro exports to Excel, CSV and JSON and also provides a Results table. It focuses on supported business identity, phone, address, website, Maps URL, ratings/reviews and available contact or social fields where the supported source data provides them.

Octoparse's free plan supports Excel, CSV, JSON, HTML and XML exports, while paid plans add more automation and integration options. That wider export and integration surface matters for data teams; it may matter less for a salesperson who ultimately needs a clean spreadsheet.

What Octoparse add-ons change the cost?

Octoparse publishes separate metered add-ons. Its current pricing page lists residential proxies at $3/GB, pay-per-result templates with variable per-result pricing, CAPTCHA solving around $1–$1.5 per thousand, crawler setup starting at $399, and managed data service starting at $599. These are optional and should only be included in your comparison if your project actually needs them.

This is another reason to price the complete workflow instead of comparing two headline subscription numbers.

Which should you choose?

Maps Hunter Pro is likely a better fit if:

  • Your main source is Google Maps.
  • You want a Chrome-based prospecting workflow.
  • You need clean lead exports rather than a crawler-building platform.
  • You prefer a simple monthly or one-time product price.
  • A salesperson, agency operator or freelancer will review the results manually.

Octoparse is likely a better fit if:

  • You scrape many websites, not just Google Maps.
  • You need cloud execution and scheduling.
  • You need APIs, cloud storage integrations or unattended recurring jobs.
  • You want to build custom no-code scraping tasks.
  • You need platform-level anti-blocking, proxies or managed scraping services.

A practical decision test

Describe your next task in one sentence. “Find dentists in several cities, review their websites and phones, then export a prospect list” points toward a focused Maps workflow. “Run multiple scrapers every night, store the output in cloud services and expose it to our software” points toward a broader platform such as Octoparse.

That distinction is more useful than counting features because it measures the cost of operating the tool, not just buying it.

Responsible prospecting

Public business information is not permission for indiscriminate outreach. Qualify prospects, use appropriate business contact channels, respect opt-outs and comply with privacy and marketing rules in the markets where you operate.

FAQ

Is Octoparse free?

Yes. Octoparse currently offers a free-forever plan with 10 tasks and local extraction, subject to its published export limits.

Does Octoparse include an API?

API access is a paid-plan capability. Octoparse documents Data Export API access in Standard and broader API capabilities in Professional.

Is Maps Hunter Pro a general web scraper?

No. It is intentionally focused on Google Maps business lead research and export.

Which is better for a sales team?

If the team's primary task is interactive Google Maps prospecting, Maps Hunter Pro is closer to that workflow. If the team needs recurring scraping across many websites and cloud automation, Octoparse offers the broader platform.
