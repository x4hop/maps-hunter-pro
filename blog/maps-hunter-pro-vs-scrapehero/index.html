Maps Hunter Pro vs ScrapeHero: Which Fits Your Google Maps Lead Workflow?

Both can help you collect Google Maps business data, but they are built for different buyers. Maps Hunter Pro is a focused Chrome prospecting tool. ScrapeHero is a broader cloud scraping platform with credits, API access, integrations, and managed scraping services.

Want a focused Google Maps prospecting tool?

If your day-to-day job is searching Google Maps, organizing business results, and exporting lead lists from Chrome, Maps Hunter Pro is designed around that narrower workflow.

The quick answer

Choose Maps Hunter Pro if you want a straightforward Chrome workflow for Google Maps lead research, prefer fixed product pricing, and do not need to build cloud scraping infrastructure.

Choose ScrapeHero if you need cloud jobs, API access, broader scraping coverage, higher-volume automation, storage integrations, or managed scraping services.

This is not a “winner takes all” comparison. ScrapeHero has capabilities Maps Hunter Pro does not try to replicate. Maps Hunter Pro is intentionally simpler and more focused. Your workflow matters more than the raw number of features.

Pricing: ScrapeHero starts cheaper, but the models are different

It would be inaccurate to say Maps Hunter Pro is always cheaper. ScrapeHero currently offers a Free cloud tier with 400 data credits, then an Intro plan at $5/month with 4,500 credits. Its Lite plan is $25/month for 30,000 credits and Starter is $50/month for 90,000 credits. Advanced tiers rise to $100, $250, $500, and $750 per month with larger credit allowances, more concurrency, longer retention and stronger integrations.

Maps Hunter Pro uses a different model: $20/month with up to 1,500 results per day, or $100 one-time for lifetime access with no commercial daily platform limit. For a person who repeatedly prospects inside Chrome, that fixed lifetime option can be easy to understand and budget. For occasional cloud scraping, ScrapeHero's free or $5 entry tier may cost less.

The right comparison is therefore not “which headline number is smaller?” but “how will you actually work?”

Price test before buying

Estimate one month of real usage. If you need API calls, recurring cloud jobs and multiple scraper types, include those benefits in the comparison. If you mainly need repeated interactive Google Maps prospecting, compare the long-term cost of that specific workflow.

Chrome workflow vs cloud workflow

Maps Hunter Pro

Maps Hunter Pro keeps the operator close to Google Maps. You search a market, collect supported business fields, review results, and export structured data to Excel, CSV, JSON, or a Results table. That is a natural fit for people who qualify leads manually and want the extraction step to remain simple.

ScrapeHero

ScrapeHero Cloud is built around jobs, credits and cloud execution. Its Google Maps products can be run without maintaining your own scraping infrastructure, and its API offering returns structured JSON for integration into software workflows. ScrapeHero also sells full-service scraping for buyers who want the vendor to handle the project.

That broader platform is valuable when scraping is only one part of a larger data pipeline. It can be unnecessary overhead when your task is simply “find local businesses, review them, and export a prospect list.”

API, automation and integrations

This is where ScrapeHero has the clear platform advantage. Its Google Maps Search API is designed for real-time structured data, and higher cloud plans include API integration. The pricing page also lists Dropbox integration on Intro, Lite and Starter tiers, while Standard and above add Amazon S3, Google Drive, role-based access control and higher API/concurrency limits.

If your specification says “our backend must request data automatically” or “we need scheduled cloud jobs and storage integrations,” ScrapeHero is closer to that requirement.

Maps Hunter Pro should be evaluated for its intended job: a focused Chrome-based lead research and export workflow. It is not positioned as a general scraping API platform.

Do you actually need a cloud scraping stack?

If not, a focused product can be easier to operate. Maps Hunter Pro keeps the process close to the research your sales or agency team is already doing.

Data, exports and enrichment

Maps Hunter Pro focuses on supported Google Maps business information and clean exports for prospecting. The current product supports Excel, CSV, JSON and an in-product Results table, with international phone formatting and available email/social fields where the supported source data provides them.

ScrapeHero's Google Maps products sit inside a broader scraping ecosystem. The company advertises cloud-based extraction, structured outputs, API access and the ability to handle other websites and datasets beyond Google Maps. Buyers who need one vendor for many unrelated scraping targets may value that breadth.

Do not assume either tool can magically produce fields a business has never published. The useful question is whether the tool gives you the fields and workflow your qualification process actually needs.

What about managed scraping?

ScrapeHero also offers a managed service separate from its self-service cloud plans. Its published pricing currently starts at $550 per website per refresh for on-demand work, while a Business managed plan starts at $199 per month per website and can include an additional setup fee. This is aimed at organizations that want a vendor to run the scraping project rather than operate a self-service lead tool.

That is not a direct Maps Hunter Pro use case. If you need engineers, QA, infrastructure and a managed delivery process, ScrapeHero's service is a different category of purchase.

Which should you choose?

Maps Hunter Pro is likely the better fit when:

  • Your main job is finding and organizing business prospects from Google Maps.
  • You want to work interactively in Chrome.
  • You prefer Excel/CSV/JSON exports over building an API pipeline.
  • You want a simple monthly or one-time lifetime product price.
  • You are an agency, freelancer or sales operator who will manually review the leads.

ScrapeHero is likely the better fit when:

  • You need cloud execution and API-based collection.
  • You want to scrape many websites or data sources beyond Google Maps.
  • You need S3, Google Drive, Dropbox or software integrations.
  • You need higher concurrency, cloud retention or team controls.
  • You want a managed scraping vendor rather than a self-service prospecting tool.

A simple decision test

Write your next real task in one sentence. If it sounds like “I need to find roofers in several cities, review the businesses, and export a lead list,” Maps Hunter Pro is aligned with that operator-led workflow.

If it sounds like “our system must call an API, run cloud jobs, retain datasets, and deliver them to storage on a schedule,” ScrapeHero is aligned with that architecture.

Choosing by workflow prevents you from paying for capabilities you do not use—or choosing a simple tool for a job that genuinely needs automation infrastructure.

Responsible use

Public business information is not blanket permission for spam. Qualify your prospects, follow applicable privacy and marketing rules, use appropriate business contact channels, and respect opt-outs.

FAQ

Is ScrapeHero cheaper than Maps Hunter Pro?

At entry level, yes: ScrapeHero has a free tier and a $5/month Intro plan. But the products use different pricing models. Maps Hunter Pro also offers a $100 one-time lifetime plan, while ScrapeHero scales through credit-based monthly tiers and separate managed services.

Which is better for an API integration?

ScrapeHero is the stronger fit if API access and cloud automation are core requirements.

Which is simpler for manual Google Maps prospecting?

Maps Hunter Pro is designed specifically around an interactive Chrome prospecting and export workflow.

Does ScrapeHero only scrape Google Maps?

No. ScrapeHero offers many scrapers, APIs and custom/managed scraping services beyond Google Maps.
