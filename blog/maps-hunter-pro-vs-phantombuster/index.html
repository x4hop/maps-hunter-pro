Maps Hunter Pro vs PhantomBuster: Which Is Better for Google Maps Leads?

If Google Maps business leads are your main goal, do you need a broad automation platform—or a focused Chrome workflow? This comparison looks at current pricing, exports, email workflows, automation and the tradeoffs that actually matter.

Maps Hunter Pro and PhantomBuster can both be part of a lead-generation workflow, but they are built around very different product ideas. Maps Hunter Pro is a focused Chrome extension for collecting and organizing supported Google Maps business data. PhantomBuster is a broader cloud automation platform that covers Google Maps alongside LinkedIn and other platforms.

That difference matters more than a long feature checklist. If you need multi-platform automations, scheduled cloud workflows and integrations, PhantomBuster offers capabilities Maps Hunter Pro is not trying to replace. If your day-to-day task is primarily Google Maps → structured business data → Excel/CSV/JSON, Maps Hunter Pro is positioned as the simpler and substantially lower-priced subscription.

Maps Hunter Pro vs PhantomBuster pricing

As of September 18, 2026, Maps Hunter Pro lists a Monthly plan at $20/month with up to 1,500 results per day and an Annual plan at $100/year with no commercial daily platform limit stated by Maps Hunter Pro.

PhantomBuster's current pricing page lists its Start plan at $69/month when billed monthly, or $56/month equivalent when billed annually ($672 billed annually). That plan includes 20 hours of execution time per month, 5 automation slots and 500 email credits per month. PhantomBuster also has higher Grow and Scale plans.

So for a user whose requirement is specifically Google Maps lead collection, Maps Hunter Pro has the lower fixed subscription price. But this is not an apples-to-apples claim that it gives more total automation: PhantomBuster's price includes a much wider automation platform.

PhantomBuster pricing can change, so verify the latest figures on its official pricing page before buying.

The biggest difference: focused browser workflow vs cloud automation

Maps Hunter Pro workflow

Maps Hunter Pro is designed around a short workflow: search Google Maps for a business category and location, run the Chrome extension, collect supported business fields, review the results and export them. It is intentionally narrow.

PhantomBuster workflow

PhantomBuster runs automations in the cloud. Its Google Maps Search Export can process search URLs, run on schedules, use proxies for location consistency and chain results into broader workflows. This is useful when Google Maps is only one step in a larger outbound system.

The tradeoff is straightforward: broader automation gives you more possibilities, while a focused extension gives you fewer layers when your job is simply extracting Maps business leads.

Business data and exports

Maps Hunter Pro supports structured business fields such as business name, category, phone, address, website, rating, review count, Google Maps URL, and available email/social fields where supported by the source data. Results can be exported to Excel, CSV, JSON or viewed in the Results table.

PhantomBuster's paid plans include unlimited exports and its broader system provides enrichment workflows, including email credits. Its free plan limits CSV exports to the first 10 rows according to its Google Maps Search Export documentation.

If you need CRM-connected enrichment across several sources, PhantomBuster has the broader toolset. If you mainly want a clean spreadsheet from Google Maps, paying for a wider automation platform may be unnecessary.

Where PhantomBuster is stronger

A useful comparison should say where the competitor wins. PhantomBuster is the stronger choice when your workflow depends on:

  • Cloud execution while your browser is closed.
  • Scheduled and repeated automations.
  • Multiple platforms beyond Google Maps.
  • Chaining automations into larger outbound workflows.
  • CRM and automation integrations.
  • Dedicated email-enrichment credits.

Maps Hunter Pro is not positioned as a replacement for all of those functions. Its advantage is focus: you do not need to buy or learn a multi-platform automation suite when your main requirement is Google Maps business research.

Who should choose Maps Hunter Pro?

Maps Hunter Pro makes the most sense for freelancers, local SEO specialists, web designers, agencies, suppliers and B2B prospectors who spend most of their research time inside Google Maps and want structured business lists without a complex cloud automation stack.

At $100/year, its annual price is also materially below PhantomBuster Start's currently listed $672 annual billing. The correct interpretation is lower cost for a narrower job—not that Maps Hunter Pro contains every PhantomBuster feature.

Choose the tool that matches the job

If you need LinkedIn, multi-platform automation and scheduled cloud workflows, PhantomBuster may justify its higher price. If Google Maps extraction is the job, Maps Hunter Pro gives you a focused workflow at a much lower subscription cost.

Bottom line

For Google Maps-first lead generation, Maps Hunter Pro is the more focused and lower-priced option based on the publicly listed prices checked on September 18, 2026. PhantomBuster is the more expansive automation platform.

The practical question is not “which product has more features?” It is “which features are you actually paying to use?” If Google Maps business extraction is your core need, start with the narrower tool and move to a broader automation suite only when your workflow genuinely requires it.
