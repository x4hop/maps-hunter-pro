Maps Hunter Pro vs Apify: Which Fits Your Google Maps Lead Workflow?

Maps Hunter Pro and Apify can both support Google Maps business research, but they solve the job in very different ways. One is a focused Chrome workflow; the other is a cloud automation platform with a marketplace of Actors. This guide helps you choose based on the work you actually need to do.

The quick answer

Choose Maps Hunter Pro if you primarily want a Chrome-based workflow for Google Maps lead research and prefer a straightforward product rather than configuring cloud scraping infrastructure.

Choose Apify if you need programmatic runs, scheduled cloud jobs, webhooks, datasets, integrations, or the flexibility to select among many Google Maps Actors and other scraping tools.

Neither answer is universally better. Apify is broader and more developer-oriented. Maps Hunter Pro is deliberately narrower. That distinction matters more than a feature-count contest.

The biggest difference: focused tool vs automation platform

Maps Hunter Pro is built around one workflow: researching businesses on Google Maps and turning the available listing information into organized lead data. It runs as a Chrome extension, so the experience stays close to the map research a salesperson, freelancer or agency operator is already doing.

Apify is a cloud platform. Its Store contains many independently maintained Actors, including multiple Google Maps scrapers. Those Actors can have different developers, prices, fields and capabilities. Comparing Maps Hunter Pro with “Apify” therefore requires an important caveat: there is no single Apify Google Maps product with one universal price and feature set.

How the pricing models differ

Maps Hunter Pro uses a subscription-style product model. That can be easier to budget when Google Maps prospecting is a regular part of your workflow.

Apify Google Maps Actors commonly use usage-based pricing. Apify's own help documentation for its Google Maps Scraper describes a base charge of $4 per 1,000 places, plus optional event charges: filters can add $0.001 per filter per place, additional place details $0.002 per place, contact details $0.002 per place, and reviews or images have their own event prices. Other community Actors in the Apify Store use different rates, so buyers should check the specific Actor rather than assume one platform-wide Google Maps price.

That difference creates two different buying questions. With Maps Hunter Pro, ask whether the subscription fits how often you prospect. With Apify, estimate the number of places, enrichment options, recurring runs and Actor-specific charges you expect to consume.

Do not compare only the headline price

A per-1,000-record rate can be excellent for occasional or automated workloads. A fixed product subscription can be easier for a human-led prospecting workflow. Estimate your actual monthly use before deciding.

What the day-to-day workflow looks like

Maps Hunter Pro

The intended workflow is visual and operator-led: define Google Maps searches, collect supported business fields, review results, remove duplicates where applicable, and export the resulting list for qualification. This is a natural fit for people who want to stay in Chrome rather than create an API request or cloud task.

Apify

Apify Actors run in the cloud. Depending on the Actor, you can provide search terms, locations, URLs or geographic inputs, then retrieve the output from an Apify dataset. Apify also exposes Actor runs through its API. This is valuable when the extraction step is part of a larger automated pipeline rather than a one-person research session.

Business fields, emails and enrichment

For either product, never assume every business will have every field. A Google Maps listing may omit a phone number or website because the business has not published one.

Apify's Google Maps ecosystem can go beyond basic listing fields. Apify's documented pricing includes optional contact-detail extraction for emails and social profiles from business websites. Several Store Actors also advertise website enrichment, reviews, photos and other extended fields. Exact capabilities depend on the Actor.

Maps Hunter Pro is strongest when you want the fields exposed by its focused Maps workflow organized into a prospecting list. If deep website enrichment, large review datasets or programmable downstream processing are mandatory requirements, evaluate the relevant Apify Actor directly.

API, scheduling and integrations

This is the clearest area where Apify has the broader platform capability. Actors can be invoked programmatically through the Apify API, and the platform is designed around datasets, cloud runs and automation. Some Actors also document integrations with tools such as Make, Zapier and Google Drive.

Maps Hunter Pro should not be purchased as though it were an Apify-style cloud scraping API. Its value proposition is the simpler Chrome prospecting workflow. If your specification starts with “our backend must call this automatically every night,” Apify is likely the more natural category of product.

Which one should you choose?

Maps Hunter Pro is likely the better fit when:

  • Your core job is Google Maps lead research.
  • You prefer working from Chrome rather than an API console.
  • You want a repeatable export workflow for sales, agency or freelance prospecting.
  • You value a focused interface over a large automation ecosystem.

Apify is likely the better fit when:

  • You need scheduled cloud runs or API-driven extraction.
  • You want to connect extraction directly to software or automation pipelines.
  • You need Actor-specific enrichment such as website contacts, reviews or images.
  • You want one platform for many scraping and automation jobs beyond Google Maps.

A practical decision test

Before paying for either option, write down one real task. For example: “Every week I need to research plumbers in five cities, keep businesses with a phone number, review them manually, and export a clean spreadsheet.” If a human will actively run and qualify that workflow, a focused browser tool can make sense.

If the requirement instead says: “Our CRM should trigger a cloud job, collect thousands of records, enrich websites, then push the dataset into another system without a person operating Chrome,” you are describing the type of infrastructure Apify is built to support.

This test prevents you from buying the tool with the longest feature list instead of the tool that matches the workflow.

Responsible use still matters

Business data can be public and still be subject to privacy, marketing and platform rules. Qualify prospects, use appropriate business contact channels, respect opt-outs, and comply with the laws that apply to your market. Do not treat an exported list as permission for indiscriminate outreach.

FAQ

Is Apify a direct Maps Hunter Pro replacement?

Not exactly. Apify is a general cloud automation and scraping platform with many Actors. Maps Hunter Pro is a focused Google Maps prospecting product.

Does Apify have one Google Maps Scraper price?

No. Different Actors can use different pricing. Apify's help documentation describes $4 per 1,000 places for its referenced Google Maps Scraper base event, while community Actors in the Store may show other rates.

Which is better for developers?

If API access, cloud execution and programmable automation are core requirements, Apify offers the more developer-oriented platform.

Which is easier for a salesperson or freelancer?

If the task is primarily interactive Google Maps prospecting and spreadsheet export, Maps Hunter Pro's focused Chrome workflow may require less infrastructure.
